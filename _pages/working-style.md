**Freestyle, not free fall.**

My advising style is flexible, candid, and student-driven. I work best with self-motivated students who take ownership of their research: take the lead in our discussions, be bold in sharing your ideas, ask for feedback, follow through on meeting TODOs, challenge my assumptions, stalk me home (okay, maybe not that last one), and most importantly, tell me when I am wrong. I will give you room to explore and be there whenever you need guidance or support, just as my advisors did for me.

The goal is not to follow instructions, but to grow into an independent researcher with your own taste and judgment. We take the work seriously.
